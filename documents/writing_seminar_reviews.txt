Course: WRIT 0150 - Writing Seminar
University: University of Pennsylvania
Source: Rate My Professors / Reddit r/UPenn

Professor: Various (Writing Program)
Rating: Varies by instructor

General student advice on Writing Seminars at Penn:
Review 1: The writing seminar you get is basically a lottery. Some instructors are incredibly helpful and give detailed feedback on every draft; others just check boxes. Before registration opens, search your assigned instructor on Rate My Professors and ask upperclassmen in your major's GroupMe if anyone has taken that section.

Review 2: All writing seminars require a research paper but the topic varies wildly by section. Some sections let you choose any topic; others have a theme (technology and society, environmental justice, etc.). Read the section description carefully before picking — the theme affects how interesting the whole semester will feel.

Review 3: The revision process is the whole point of the course. You write, get feedback, revise, and repeat. Students who skip drafts or treat them as optional always regret it at the final paper stage. Turn in every draft even if it's rough.

Review 4: Office hours with your writing instructor are more useful in this course than in any other Penn course. They can tell you exactly what's missing in your argument before you submit the graded version. Use them every assignment.

Review 5: Penn writing seminars are graded more harshly than most courses. An A- is a good grade here. Don't expect the same GPA boost you'd get from a lecture course. The instructors take academic integrity very seriously — plagiarism cases go to the Honor Council regularly.

Review 6: Some sections meet in the morning which is brutal for freshmen still adjusting to college schedules. If you have a choice, pick an afternoon section. Also: the seminar you're assigned freshman year is non-negotiable — you can request a switch but it's rarely granted.
