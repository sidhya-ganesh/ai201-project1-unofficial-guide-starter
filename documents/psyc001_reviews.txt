Course: PSYC 0001 - Introduction to Psychology
University: University of Pennsylvania
Source: Rate My Professors / PennCourseReview

Professor: Alan Gelperin
Rating: 3.9/5
Review 1: Intro Psych at Penn is a big lecture course (200+ students) and Gelperin is solid if not spectacular. His lectures cover the textbook closely so you can follow along in either the readings or his slides. Exams are multiple choice and focus on definitions, key studies, and experimental results. Memorization-heavy.

Review 2: The textbook is expensive but necessary — a lot of exam questions come directly from it, including things he doesn't cover in lecture. Buy it or find a PDF online. The curve is moderate. Midterms are more predictable than the final.

Review 3: He's enthusiastic about the material and occasionally brings in demos or videos which break up the lecture nicely. Not the most interactive course but it's a solid survey of the field. Good choice if you want an easy-ish science distribution credit.

Professor: Angela Duckworth
Rating: 4.9/5
Review 1: Angela Duckworth is a celebrity in the field (she wrote the book Grit) and teaching with her is extraordinary. She brings in world-class guest speakers, runs live experiments during lecture, and is somehow accessible despite being famous. She remembers student names in a 200-person lecture. A once-in-a-college-lifetime experience.

Review 2: Duckworth's course is not easy — the readings are rigorous and she expects you to engage with primary sources, not just textbook summaries. But her passion for the subject is unreal. She genuinely wants to understand what motivates human behavior and she makes you care about it too.

Review 3: Exams test application and synthesis, not just memorization. She'll give you a scenario and ask you to apply three different psychological frameworks to it. If you just memorize definitions you will struggle. Think about how the concepts connect.

Review 4: She is responsive on Ed Discussion and her TAs are well-trained. The final paper is an opportunity to genuinely explore something you find interesting — take it seriously and you'll be proud of what you produce.
