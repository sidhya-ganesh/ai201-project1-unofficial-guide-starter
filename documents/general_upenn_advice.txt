University: University of Pennsylvania
Source: Reddit r/UPenn / Penn student Discord / Class Facebook groups

General UPenn Course and Professor Advice:

Registration Tips:
Review 1: Path@Penn is the course registration system and it is brutal. Seniors register first, then juniors, then sophomores, then freshmen. As a freshman you will not get your top choices — plan for your second and third options. Use the waitlist aggressively, especially in the first two weeks of the semester when people drop courses.

Review 2: Always show up to the first lecture of a waitlisted course. Professors sometimes let in students who attend even if the official roster is full. Sitting in for two weeks while on the waitlist has gotten many students off it. Don't just wait for the system to update.

Review 3: PennCourseReview is more reliable than Rate My Professors for Penn-specific courses. It uses data from past students who actually took the course and aggregates scores for course quality, difficulty, and instructor quality separately.

Review 4: Penn's shopping period is the first two weeks of the semester. You can attend any course during this time without being officially enrolled. Use this to test professors before committing. Some courses feel completely different in person than they do on paper.

Exam and Grading Tips:
Review 5: Many Penn courses curve — but not all. Before assuming there's a curve, check the syllabus or ask the professor directly in the first week. Never assume a curve exists and then be surprised when it doesn't.

Review 6: Penn has a grade replacement policy for some courses if you retake them, but the original grade stays on your transcript. Check the registrar's website before retaking a course expecting it to disappear.

Review 7: Office hours are the most underused resource at Penn. Most Penn professors see very few students at office hours outside of exam weeks. Going regularly, especially in large lecture courses, is one of the best ways to stand out and get useful help.

Review 8: The Academic Support Programs office (in College Hall) offers tutoring and academic coaching for free. Almost nobody uses it until they're in trouble. Use it proactively, especially in your first semester.

Review 9: Penn's honor code applies to all courses. Collaboration rules vary by course — some allow group work on homework, others do not. Read the course policy carefully at the start of the semester and when in doubt, ask the professor. Honor code violations go on your academic record permanently.

Review 10: Finals period at Penn is intense. The Van Pelt library is open 24 hours during finals week but gets crowded fast. The Weigle Information Commons is a better bet for quiet space. Huntsman Hall study rooms can be reserved online and are excellent but book them at least a week in advance.
